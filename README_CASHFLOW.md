## 🎯 The Problem

**82% of rural micro enterprises fail within 3 years due to cash flow crises they never saw coming.**

63 million rural micro enterprises in India face:
- 🚫 **No prediction tools** - Can't forecast when money will run out
- 💸 **₹15,000 Cr annual losses** from preventable cash flow problems  
- 📉 **Reactive management** - Discover problems when it's too late
- ❌ **No risk visibility** - Don't know which vulnerabilities will hurt most
- 🏦 **Can't access credit** - Banks need financial projections they can't provide

**When a rural shop owner's cash runs out during low season, when a farmer can't pay loan EMI because receivables are stuck, when a small manufacturer can't buy raw materials - these are predictable tragedies.**
